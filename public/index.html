How to use this tool

Enter the Order number, pick the foam recipe (choose the exact one if it comes in two), then the depth, size, any finishing extras, and the carriage. The price builds on the right. Override the Agreed price if the actual figure differs, then use the buttons below the price:

Save Order

Saves this order to the Saved Orders log at the bottom of the page. An order number is required first. It records the mattress, every charge, the calculated and agreed price and the date — so any order can be checked back later.

Clear

Empties the form for the next order — resets the build, size, depth, extras and price override. You must Save the current order first: Clear is blocked and shows a warning until the order is saved — or, if the entry is wrong, use discard it in that warning to clear without saving. It never deletes anything already saved.

Copy

Copies the quote as text to paste into an email or message. Nothing is saved.

Print

Prints just the quote (form hidden). Use your printer, or choose “Save as PDF” to keep a copy.
