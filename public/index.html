How to use this tool — tap to open

Work through one supplier invoice at a time: set the invoice number once, then run each order on it through the calculator, checking what the supplier charged against what it should be. It auto-fills as much as it can from the order.

1 · Invoice & order

Invoice number — set it once; it stays put while you work the whole invoice.
Order number — type or pick it and the tool pulls the order in: foam recipe, supplier code, depth, size, shape diagram and link/join all fill themselves from your order app.
Invoiced Price — what the supplier actually charged for this line. Leave it blank if it matches the calculated price.

2 · Pricing (mostly automatic)

Foam recipe — matched from the order's supplier code and shown as a chip; hit Change to override, or pick it by hand.
Supplier code & depth — filled from the code; watch for the amber tufting flag on Imperial.
Size — worked out from the order's A & B measurements.
Finishing & extras — drag ⠿ to reorder; the Link / join reminder sticks to the top as you scroll (✕ to dismiss once applied).
Carriage — courier or manual.

3 · Banners you may see

💬 Quoted price agreed (bespoke jobs) — the calculator can't price these, so the agreed quote is the price. No recipe needed: just enter what the supplier invoiced and Save.
⚠ Supplier credit due — a credit to reconcile later.
Link / join and tufting — build details the drawing alone doesn't show.

4 · Check & reconcile

In the log, the Invoiced price shows green when it matches the calculated (or quoted) price and red when it doesn't.
Enter the Invoice total (the gross figure inc VAT) and the running note tells you if the whole invoice balances — it adds the supplier's extra line (Southern: automatic 12% foam surcharge · Mattressshire: boxes at £8 each) plus 20% VAT.
🧮 Calculate invoice lays the whole thing out like the paper invoice — every line, the extra, net, VAT, total — and shows a Winner verdict: whether you or the supplier came out ahead on the price differences, and by how much.

Buttons
Save & Next

The one you'll use most. Saves the order and jumps straight to the next one, keeping the invoice number set. No re-typing, no clearing.

Save Order

Saves the order but leaves the form as-is so you can review it.

Clear

Empties the form to start a fresh invoice. Blocked until the current order is saved — or use discard it to clear an entry without saving. Never deletes saved orders.

Copy

Copies the quote as text to paste into an email or message.

Print

Prints just the quote (or “Save as PDF”).

Saved Orders (bottom of the page)

Every saved order lands here, grouped under its invoice. Click an invoice header to expand its lines; the header shows the item count, net & inc-VAT totals, whether it balances, how many prices mismatch, and the 🏆 Winner chip. Click any row for the full breakdown, or Export CSV for your records. Orders are shared between you and the calculator, and survive a refresh.
